About me

I’m pursuing a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence with a concentration in computer vision at Northeastern University’s College of Engineering in Boston, graduating in May 2028.

Before grad school I spent two years at IBM testing enterprise telecom products for AT&T and DIRECTV. Testing taught me that a passing result means little until you know how it was checked and what it missed. In my ML work that shows up as careful evaluation: held-out test sets, honest baselines, field tests on data the model has never seen, and a written list of what I’d question in my own results.

Right now I’m leading evaluation and statistics on OpsPilot, a team research project on making LLM agents’ incident diagnoses more trustworthy, and building GridPulse AI, an EV charging intelligence platform, on my own.
